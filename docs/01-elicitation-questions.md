### Question categories for the interview


**A. Business goals / the Epic**  
The goal is to find the answer to _"What major problem are we trying to solve?"_ (_L2, "Epic"_).
- **==Lack of communication with customers. Hard to track customers needs.==**
- **==Random customers visitings(Hard to estimate regular income, Hard to manage time).==**

- If this website works perfectly, what changes in your daily work six months from now?
	- My dad can manage his time, like when the customer came to his hospital, he can check his schedule and give another time to the customer. 
	- He can post his emergency hour changes to let customers know.
	- He doesn't have to be nervous every time even there is no customers.
- What do you spend the most time on right now that you wish you didn't? (Answering the same phone questions? Explaining hours? Directions?)
- How do new clients find you today?

**B. Personas — the "who"**  
You need name, role, context, goals/expectations, and frustrations/pain points (_L2, "Persona"_).

- Describe your three most common types of client.
- What do people ask you on the phone before they've ever visited?
- What makes someone choose you over the vet two blocks away?

**C. Functional requirements — services offered**

- What services do you offer, and how would you group them?
- Is there anything you _don't_ treat, that people wrongly assume you do?
- Do you want appointment booking online, or is that a problem you don't want?

_(That last one matters enormously — online booking turns a static site into a system with state, a database, authentication, and notifications. Ask it early.)_

**D. Content ownership — the hidden killer**

- Who will update the site when hours change or a service is added? You? Me? Forever?
- Do you have photos of the clinic and staff? Professional or phone camera?
- Who writes the Korean text — do you want me to translate anything, or will you write it?

**E. Non-functional requirements**  
Probe indirectly; he won't use these words.

- Will people mostly find this on their phone or a computer? _(→ mobile-first, responsiveness)_
	- Web searching(Naver) from their smartphone.
- If someone's dog is sick at 11pm, what's the first thing they need to see? _(→ emergency contact prominence, performance)_
    - Emergency contact to navigate emergency clinic or hospital. (Still waiting from dad)
- Are there Korean regulations on what a veterinary clinic may advertise or claim? _(→ legal constraints)_
    - Find regulation from Korea

- If a client submits their name and phone number, where does that go? _(→ security, and Korean personal data law)_

**F. Constraints**

- Budget for hosting and a domain name?
- Any deadline? (A clinic anniversary, a new service launch?)
- Does the clinic already have a Naver Place listing, Instagram, or KakaoTalk channel?
